A letter for you

Happy Birthday, my favourite person ♡

I don't really know how to fit everything I want to say into one little website, but I still wanted to try.

You have become such a beautiful part of my everyday life. The random conversations, the calls, the silly moments, the little things you say without even thinking about them — somehow all of them mean much more to me than they probably seem.

I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope you laugh a lot, feel proud of yourself more often, and have plenty of moments where you realise just how loved you are.

And whenever life gets a little heavy, I hope you remember that you don't always have to have everything figured out. You can simply take a breath, rest, and start again.

Today is your day, so please smile a little extra, eat something nice, enjoy yourself, and let yourself be celebrated.

Just a reminder

You don't have to be productive every second of the day. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to have a bad day. And you are still completely worth celebrating on every single one of them.

✦

For your smile

I hope something unexpectedly funny happens today. Like laughing at something that isn't even that funny and then laughing even harder because you're already laughing.

🌷

For your future

I hope the girl you become keeps the softness, kindness and little spark that makes you you. You don't need to become someone else to be amazing.

For the tiny wins

Finished something difficult? Proud of yourself for once? Got through a weird day? Good. It counts. Tiny wins are still wins.

♡

For today

No serious mission. No giant speech. Just go enjoy your birthday and remember that somewhere, someone is very happy that you exist.
